Field orders

How Declass works

WW2 Dispatches posts cards from history every day. Each one comes with a classified envelope here. Declassify it, turn the cards, and build your dossier.

Daily envelopes

  • An envelope comes out with each card the account posts, usually in the morning (10:00 a.m. ET) and the evening (7:00 p.m. ET). Big anniversaries can bring more.
  • Each stays open for 24 hours, so wherever you live you have a full day to open it.
  • When you see a new post on our accounts, that's your signal: the envelope is out.

Declassifying one

  • Choose one of the three sealed envelopes. Each holds three cards: the day's dispatch and two from the archive.
  • Tap a card to turn it over, then swipe to the next.
  • The cards are yours the moment the envelope opens. If you leave early, they're already in your dossier, and a button on the home screen takes you back to turn them. A new envelope stays locked until you do.

Finishes

  • Every card comes in one of four finishes, set by luck: Copper (most cards), Silver (about 1 in 5), Gold (about 1 in 20), and Top Secret (about 1 in 400, numbered worldwide).
  • Each finish is its own slot. A copper and a gold of the same card are two different cards in your dossier. You won't see what a finish looks like until you pull it.
  • The odds are the same in every envelope, for everyone.

Living Cards

  • When a day has real archival film, that card also exists as a Living Card: the same card with the film playing on it. They turn up in about 1 in 6 archive cards, and they come in better finishes.

Name This cards

  • Our Name This puzzles join the archive once their answer is public. Turn one over, take a guess, then tap again for the answer.

Streaks and priority envelopes

  • Open at least one envelope a day (Eastern time) to keep your streak going. Miss a day and it starts over.
  • Every third day of your streak earns a priority envelope: three cards from anywhere in the archive, all the way back to the first dispatch. It never expires.

Your dossier

  • Browse your cards by series, by year, or all at once. Sets collect a month's files for a stamp, and collections (a series, a year, a theater, a nation) for pins at 25, 50, and 100 cards.
  • The four small squares under each card show which finishes you hold, and ▶ means a Living Card exists for it.
  • Tap any card to read its story, see its finishes, and every time you've pulled it.
