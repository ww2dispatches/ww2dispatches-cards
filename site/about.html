About

WW2 Dispatches

The Second World War, told on the day it happened.

Every day we post what happened on this date during the war, from the road to war in the 1930s to the surrender in 1945. Real photographs and film from the time, short stories you can read in a minute, and longer ones when a day deserves it.

What you'll find

The daily dispatches

Five posts a day, each tied to something that happened on today's date. Every dispatch carries one of four labels.

  • Battle LinesBattles and military operations.
  • Home FrontRationing, factories, and everyday people.
  • BlueprintRadar, codebreakers, and the engineering that won it.
  • StatecraftPolitics and diplomacy, from Munich to Yalta.

Day by DayA short dispatch each morning at 6:30 while the series runs, as if it were happening now.

How we work

  • Checked before it postsEvery date and fact is checked against sources before anything goes out, and each Campaign Series day lists its sources.
  • Real photographs and filmWe use archival photos and film from the time, most of them public domain, and credit them with every post. When no photo exists, we say so.
  • Corrections welcomeIf we get something wrong, tell us and we fix it.
