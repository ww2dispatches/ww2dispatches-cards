Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

The short version: this site doesn't track you, and it doesn't collect anything unless you choose to sign up or write to us.

When you visit

ww2dispatches.com has no accounts, no ads, no trackers, no analytics, and no cookies of our own. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, handles your internet address to deliver the page, as every web host does. The page also loads its fonts from Google Fonts and its card images from GitHub, and those services see your internet address when they send them.

The Weekly Dispatch

If you enter your email in the signup box, it goes straight to Substack, which runs our newsletter. We use it only to send the newsletter, and every issue has a one click unsubscribe. Substack's own privacy policy covers how it stores your address.

Supporting us on Ko-fi

Ko-fi handles the payment. It may share your supporter name, your email, and any message you leave with us; we use them only to thank you.

Other links

X, Instagram, Threads, Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok are their own services with their own privacy policies. We only see what you choose to do there, like a follow or a comment.

Writing to us

If you email contact@ww2dispatches.com, we use your message only to reply.

Children

This site is for a general audience and isn't aimed at children under 13.

Changes

If anything here changes, this page will say so and show the date.
