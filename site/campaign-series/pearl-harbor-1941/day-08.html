Pearl HarborDay 8 of 12

Blueprint. Wednesday, 3 December 1941 · Washington, D.C.

Japan's embassies burn their codes

Embassies burn their codes when they expect a war. Today Washington warns its admirals that Japan's embassies are doing exactly that.

The Navy tells Admiral Kimmel and the Asiatic Fleet that Tokyo has sent "categoric and urgent instructions" to its posts in Hong Kong, Singapore, Batavia, Manila, Washington and London to destroy most of their codes and ciphers.

Washington can read Japan's secret diplomatic traffic thanks to a copy of the Purple cipher machine that Army codebreakers built without ever seeing the real one.
