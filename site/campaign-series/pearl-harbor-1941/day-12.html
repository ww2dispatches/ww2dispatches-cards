Battle Lines. Sunday, 7 December 1941 · Off Pearl Harbor, Oahu

Five midget submarines in the dark

Ten miles off Pearl Harbor, in the black hours before dawn, five large Japanese submarines let go of their passengers.

Each has carried a two man midget submarine on its deck. The first slips away around midnight. The last leaves at about 3:30, steering for the lights of Honolulu. Their orders are to get inside the harbor and wait.

To the north, the six carriers run south through the dark toward their launch point.

Photo: Midget submarine HA 19 aground on an eastern Oahu beach after the attack, December 1941. U.S. Navy, public domain.
