Statecraft. Thursday, 27 November 1941 · Washington, D.C.

Washington sends a war warning

The message reaching Admiral Husband Kimmel in Hawaii today opens with words no fleet commander wants to read: "This despatch is to be considered a war warning."

Admiral Harold Stark, the Chief of Naval Operations, tells Kimmel and the Asiatic Fleet that talks with Japan have ceased and an aggressive move is expected within days. He names the likely targets: the Philippines, Thailand's Kra Peninsula, or Borneo.

Hawaii is not on the list. The Army sends its own warning to General Walter Short, who orders an alert against sabotage.
