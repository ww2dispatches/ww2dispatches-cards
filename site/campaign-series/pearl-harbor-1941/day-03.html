Battle Lines. Friday, 28 November 1941 · Pearl Harbor, Hawaii

Enterprise sails under war conditions

A dozen Marine fighter pilots take off from Ewa this morning thinking it is a training flight. Within hours they are aboard the carrier Enterprise, and Hawaii is falling behind them.

Admiral William Halsey's task force sails from Pearl Harbor to carry the Wildcats of squadron VMF 211 to Wake Island, nearly 2,500 miles west. Once clear of land, the captain issues Battle Order No. 1: "The Enterprise is now operating under war conditions."

It closes with one plain line: "Steady nerves and stout hearts are needed now."
