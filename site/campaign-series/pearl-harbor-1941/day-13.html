Statecraft. Monday, 8 December 1941 · Washington, D.C.

A date which will live in infamy

The country wakes this morning to the news of yesterday. In under two hours, Japanese planes sank or damaged all eight battleships at Pearl Harbor and wrecked the airfields around it. More than 2,300 Americans are dead, 1,177 of them aboard the Arizona. Japan lost 29 planes and all five midget submarines.

At 12:30 in Washington, President Roosevelt stands before Congress: "Yesterday, December 7th, 1941, a date which will live in infamy, the United States of America was suddenly and deliberately attacked by naval and air forces of the Empire of Japan."

Within the hour the Senate votes 82 to 0 for war and the House 388 to 1. At 4:10 that afternoon, the President signs the declaration.
