Pearl HarborDay 5 of 12

Statecraft. Sunday, 30 November 1941 · Tokyo, Japan

A prince warns his brother

The Emperor's younger brother brings him a message the admirals have not said out loud: the navy would rather not fight America.

Prince Takamatsu, a commander on the Naval General Staff, tells Emperor Hirohito that the navy hopes to avoid war. That afternoon the Emperor summons Prime Minister Tojo, then calls in Navy Minister Shigetaro Shimada and Admiral Osami Nagano, Chief of the Naval General Staff.

For twenty five minutes the two admirals answer him. They assure him the navy is prepared. The Imperial Conference will meet tomorrow.
