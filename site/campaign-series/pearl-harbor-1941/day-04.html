Pearl HarborDay 4 of 12

Statecraft. Saturday, 29 November 1941 · Tokyo, Japan

Old prime ministers voice doubts

Eight men who once led Japan are called to the Imperial Palace today. It is their one chance to speak before the decision is made.

The former prime ministers question the government on the talks with America and on Japan's strength for war. After lunch the Emperor hears them himself. Most voice doubts, but none says plainly that war is wrong.

Two retired admirals, Keisuke Okada and Mitsumasa Yonai, warn that Japan's war supplies would drain fast and cripple the navy. Prime Minister Hideki Tojo is not moved.
