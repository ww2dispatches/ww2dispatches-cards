Battle Lines. Thursday, 30 November 1939 · Helsinki · Finland

Bombs fall on Helsinki

Moscow radio told the world its planes had dropped only bread on Helsinki. In Helsinki, the city centre was on fire.

30 November 1939. The Soviet invasion of Finland began that morning. Helsinki's first sirens sounded at 9:15 a.m., and the worst raid came between 3:00 and 4:20 p.m., when bombs hit the city centre and left a wing of the Technical University blazing. Yle counts 91 people killed in the day's raids.

The Finns answered with sarcasm. They called the Soviet cluster bombs Molotov's bread baskets, and named their petrol bottle bomb the Molotov cocktail, a drink to go with the food.

Finland fought on alone until March 1940.
