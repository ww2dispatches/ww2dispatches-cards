Blueprint. Friday, 1 December 1944 · Western Front · Europe

The Cub that hunted tanks

Before the war, Charles Carpenter taught high school history in Illinois. In 1944 he bolted six bazookas to a tiny Army spotter plane and went after German armor.

1 December 1944. Signal Corps cameramen filmed men of the 104th Infantry Division Artillery fitting a rocket launcher under the wing of an L-4 and firing it in flight. The idea had spread from pilots like Major Carpenter, who scouted for Patton's 4th Armored Division in a Cub he named Rosie the Rocketer.

The L-4 was the Army's version of Piper's civilian J-3 Cub. Its real job was spotting targets for the artillery, and 5,549 were built. Carpenter's own plane survived the war and was later restored to flying condition.
