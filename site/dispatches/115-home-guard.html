Home Front. Sunday, 3 December 1944 · London · Britain

The Home Guard stands down

More than 1.5 million Britons volunteered to fight an invasion that never came. On this Sunday they paraded for the last time.

3 December 1944. The Home Guard stood down with final parades across the country. In Hyde Park about 7,000 officers and men, from every unit in Britain, marched past King George VI, their Colonel in Chief, with the Queen and the two princesses beside him. At 9 p.m. the King broadcast his thanks.

It was not without cost: 1,206 members were killed on duty or died of wounds, many from bombs and rockets. That same autumn Germany went the other way, ordering males from 16 to 60 into the Volkssturm.
