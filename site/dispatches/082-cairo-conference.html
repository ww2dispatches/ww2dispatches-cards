Statecraft. Monday, 22 November 1943 · Cairo · Egypt

The Cairo Conference opens

Look closely at the woman on the right. In the talks between Roosevelt and her husband, every word passed through her.

22 November 1943. Roosevelt flew into Cairo past the Sphinx and the Pyramids, and that afternoon Churchill, Generalissimo Chiang Kai-shek and Madame Chiang, Soong Mei-ling, called on him. The conference, code named Sextant, met at the Mena House Hotel at the foot of the Pyramids.

This group was posed three days later, at noon on 25 November, in the garden of the villa where Roosevelt was staying. Churchill grumbled that the staff talks were "disturbed and distressingly confused by Chinese matters."

The Cairo Declaration of 1 December promised Manchuria, Formosa and the Pescadores back to China.
