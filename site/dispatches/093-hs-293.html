Blueprint. Friday, 26 November 1943 · Off Bougie · Algeria

The bomb that sank the Rohna

Hans Dochtermann released one glide bomb over a convoy off Algeria. It killed 1,015 Americans, and the full account did not come out until 1967.

26 November 1943. Luftwaffe He 177s attacked convoy KMF 26 off Bougie late that afternoon and launched a wave of Hs 293s. Dochtermann's struck the port side of HMT Rohna, a troopship carrying American soldiers. 1,138 men died, the largest loss of U.S. troops at sea from enemy action in a single incident.

The Hs 293 was a small winged missile with a 10 second rocket boost and a warhead built on a 500 kg bomb. A crewman guided it over a radio link while watching a light on its tail.

USS Pioneer and two other ships pulled 819 survivors from the dark sea.

Henschel Hs 293 A-1 glide bomb
Carried byHe 111, He 177, Fw 200, Do 217
Weight1,045 kg (2,304 lb)
WingspanAbout 3.1 m (10 ft)
WarheadBuilt on a 500 kg bomb
GuidanceRadio link, steered by joystick
