Blueprint. Tuesday, 6 November 1945 · USS Wake Island · at sea

The first jet lands aboard

His engine died on the approach to the carrier. So he landed on his other engine, the jet in the tail.

6 November 1945. Ensign Jake C. West brought a Ryan FR-1 Fireball aboard the escort carrier USS Wake Island. It was the first jet powered landing on a carrier.

The Fireball had a radial engine and propeller in the nose and a General Electric turbojet in the tail. The propeller handled takeoffs and landings, and pilots normally shut the jet down to land.

When West's radial failed, he feathered the dead propeller and restarted the jet, which could take about 30 seconds, while his plane sank toward the sea. His tailhook caught the last wire.
