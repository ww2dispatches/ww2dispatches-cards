Home Front. Monday, 27 November 1944 · Fauld, Staffordshire · England

Britain's biggest explosion

Upper Castle Hayes Farm was there at 11:10 that morning. A minute later the farmhouse, its people and its animals were gone.

27 November 1944. The RAF's underground bomb store at Fauld in Staffordshire, in the tunnels of an old gypsum mine, blew up. About 4,000 tonnes of explosives went off, generally counted as the largest explosion ever on British soil. It was heard in Birmingham and Coventry, and a burst reservoir flooded the plaster works next door.

About 70 people died, among them Leading Aircraftman William Deuchars, 38, a painter from Crieff. The secret inquiry pointed to a worker taking a detonator out of a live bomb with a brass chisel. Its report stayed hidden until 1974.
