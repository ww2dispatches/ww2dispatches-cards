Blueprint. Saturday, 20 November 1943 · Betio, Tarawa Atoll · Gilbert Islands

Fortress Betio

Rear Admiral Keiji Shibasaki is said to have told his men that a million men could not take Tarawa in a hundred years. It fell in about three days.

20 November 1943. Shibasaki's garrison, including about 1,200 Korean laborers, had ringed Betio with log and coral barriers, wire, mines and concrete obstacles, and built about 500 pillboxes and strongpoints. His plan was to destroy any landing at the water's edge.

That morning his four British made 8 inch Vickers guns fired the battle's opening shots. American battleships silenced them quickly. The bombardment also cut his above ground telephone wires, so he could not organize a counterattack on the first night.

Sources disagree on when he died, giving 20, 21 or 22 November.
