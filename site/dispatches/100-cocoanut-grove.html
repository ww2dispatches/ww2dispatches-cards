Home Front. Saturday, 28 November 1942 · Boston · USA

Fire at the Cocoanut Grove

At 10:45 p.m. a Boston Police call reached the Navy Yard asking for help. This is the log page that recorded it.

28 November 1942. Fire broke out at the Cocoanut Grove nightclub at about 10:15 p.m., with more than 1,000 people inside a club rated for about 460. The main entrance was a single revolving door, and it jammed. 492 people died, the deadliest nightclub fire in history.

The Navy Yard sent three Marine trucks and six station wagons with hospital corpsmen. At Massachusetts General Hospital, Oliver Cope's team tried a new burn treatment, and what they learned shaped burn care for decades. The fire also brought outward opening doors and lit exit signs into fire codes across the country.
