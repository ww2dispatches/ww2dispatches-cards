Battle Lines. Friday, 4 December 1942 · Naples · Italy

The cruiser that rolled over

The light cruiser Muzio Attendolo had already lost her bow once that year. In Naples harbor her crew fought the flooding for hours. Then a false alarm sent them to shelter.

4 December 1942. U.S. B-24 Liberators flying from Egypt bombed Naples in the first American raid on Italy. One or two bombs struck Muzio Attendolo amidships, and water poured in below the waterline.

At 9.17 p.m. the alarm took her damage control parties away for more than an hour. At about 10.19 p.m. she rolled over and settled on the bottom at her moorings. The Italian navy's veterans' association puts her dead at 188.

Bombs also fell on the city, and estimates of the civilian dead range from about 150 to about 900.
