Blueprint. Tuesday, 28 November 1944 · Yokosuka · Japan

The supercarrier that never arrived

Captain Toshio Abe asked for more time. Most of his ship's watertight doors were not even fitted. He was ordered to sail anyway.

28 November 1944. Shinano left Yokosuka for Kure with three destroyers. She was the heaviest aircraft carrier yet built, begun as the third Yamato class battleship and finished as a carrier after Midway, and so secret that she was never officially photographed while being built.

That night USS Archerfish found her on radar. Early on 29 November at least four torpedoes hit her starboard side. Flooding spread through untested compartments, and she capsized at 10:57 a.m., ten days after commissioning. Abe chose to go down with her.

U.S. intelligence at first doubted a carrier had been sunk at all.
