Statecraft. Tuesday, 30 November 1943 · Tehran · Iran

A birthday and a promise

On his 69th birthday, Winston Churchill sat at dinner between the two men he needed most. That same day, Stalin finally got the promise he had pressed for since 1941.

30 November 1943, Tehran. Before lunch Roosevelt read Stalin the Allied chiefs' decision: the invasion of France, Operation Overlord, would come in May 1944. Stalin said he was greatly satisfied, and pledged a Red Army offensive at the same time.

That night 33 guests filled Churchill's table, with Roosevelt on his right and Stalin on his left. Churchill toasted "Stalin the Great." The final toast was Stalin's own, and it went to American production: without the planes from America, he said, the war would have been lost.
