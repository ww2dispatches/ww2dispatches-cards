Blueprint. Saturday, 4 December 1943 · Kwajalein · Marshall Islands

The fighter that took punishment

Ensign Byron Johnson was trapped in his burning fighter with the canopy jammed. Lt. Walter Chewning ran to the plane, forced the canopy open and dragged him out.

4 December 1943. Their carrier, USS Enterprise, and five others sent their Hellcats over Kwajalein and Wotje, where they shot down 19 Japanese fighters. Built to beat the Zero, the F6F carried heavy cockpit armor, self sealing fuel tanks and a 2,000 horsepower engine.

The photo was taken on Enterprise three and a half weeks earlier, on 10 November. Johnson escaped without significant injury, and the picture became one of the best known of the war.

Grumman built 12,275 Hellcats, credited with a victory ratio of 19 to 1.
