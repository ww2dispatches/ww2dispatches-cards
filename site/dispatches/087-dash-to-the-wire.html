Battle Lines. Monday, 24 November 1941 · Sidi Rezegh · Libya

Rommel's dash to the wire

Rommel's plan was to drive straight past the British into their rear and break their nerve. By nightfall he was lost on the wrong side of the wire.

24 November 1941. A day after a costly win at Sidi Rezegh, Rommel overruled the Afrika Korps commander, Ludwig Crüwell, who wanted to stay and finish the British armor. At 10 a.m. he set off at the head of the 21st Panzer Division for the Egyptian frontier. The columns covered about 60 miles in six hours. British supply trucks and headquarters fled east, and the Eighth Army commander's aircraft was shelled as it took off.

But General Auchinleck ordered the attack on Tobruk to go on. Within days the panzers had to turn back, and on 7 to 8 December Rommel lifted the siege of Tobruk.
