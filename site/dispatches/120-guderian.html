Battle Lines. Friday, 5 December 1941 · Tula · Soviet Union

Guderian calls off Tula

That morning Heinz Guderian still believed he could take Tula. By evening he was asking to retreat.

5 December 1941. The night before, the temperature had fallen to 25 below zero Fahrenheit. Tanks would not start, and the oil in the guns' recoil mechanisms had congealed. Guderian's Second Panzer Army had wrapped a deep bulge around Tula, held by the Soviet 50th Army and a regiment of the city's workers.

Field Marshal von Bock approved the withdrawal that night. Tula never fell; its defence had lasted 43 days.

When Hitler's stand fast order arrived, Guderian refused to pass it on. At Christmas he was relieved of his command.
