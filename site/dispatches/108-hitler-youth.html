Home Front. Tuesday, 1 December 1936 · Berlin · Germany

A law for Germany's children

Baldur von Schirach was handed the young people of Germany by law. Ten years later he stood in a courtroom and called the guilt his own.

1 December 1936. Hitler signed the Law on the Hitler Youth. It charged the movement with raising the young, alongside home and school, in the spirit of National Socialism, and made Schirach Youth Leader of the German Reich, answering directly to Hitler. Jewish children were barred.

Membership had already passed 5 million. A 1939 decree made service a legal duty for every young German from 10 to 18.

By 1945 boys of 16 were fighting in the Volkssturm. At Nuremberg Schirach said: "The guilt is mine in that I educated the youth of Germany for a man who murdered by the millions." He served 20 years in Spandau.
