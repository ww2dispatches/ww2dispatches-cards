Battle Lines. Tuesday, 25 November 1941 · Off Sidi Barrani · Egypt

U-331 sinks HMS Barham

A U-boat slipped through the destroyer screen, fired four torpedoes from 375 meters, then broke the surface right beside the British fleet.

It dove out of control to about 265 meters, far below its rated depth of 150, and survived.

25 November 1941. U-331, under Oberleutnant zur See Hans-Diedrich von Tiesenhausen, 28, found the Mediterranean Fleet's battleships off the Egyptian coast. A sonar contact on HMS Jervis was dismissed. At 16:25 three torpedoes struck HMS Barham on her port side. She rolled over, and about four minutes later her after magazines exploded. 862 of the 1,311 men aboard died.

Tiesenhausen could report only a hit. The Admiralty announced the loss on 27 January 1942, and he received the Knight's Cross that same day.
