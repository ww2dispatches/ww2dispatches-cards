Battle Lines. Sunday, 21 November 1943 · Betio, Tarawa Atoll · Gilbert Islands

Shoup reports from Betio

Two days into Tarawa, the colonel running the fight sent his general a report that ended in a line Marines still quote.

21 November 1943. Late that afternoon Col. David M. Shoup's situation report from Betio reached Maj. Gen. Julian C. Smith. It ended: "Casualties many; percentage dead not known; combat efficiency: We are winning."

Shoup, 38, was the junior colonel in the 2nd Marine Division, given the assault only when another commander fell ill. A shell fragment had wounded him on the first day. By the second afternoon, Marines had crossed the airfield to the south shore and fresh battalions were landing.

He received the Medal of Honor, the only one of Tarawa's four recipients who lived to receive it. In 1959 he became the 22nd Commandant of the Marine Corps.

Casualties many; percentage dead not known; combat efficiency: We are winning.

Col. David M. Shoup, situation report from Betio, 21 November 1943
