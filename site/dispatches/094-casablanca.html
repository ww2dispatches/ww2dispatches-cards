Home Front. Thursday, 26 November 1942 · New York · United States

Casablanca opens in New York

Warner Bros. had planned to hold Casablanca until June 1943. Then Allied troops landed at Casablanca, and the studio moved fast.

26 November 1942. On Thanksgiving Day the film opened at the Hollywood Theatre in New York, just eighteen days after American and British troops came ashore in French North Africa. Humphrey Bogart played Rick Blaine, running a nightclub in Vichy controlled Casablanca.

The luck held. The general release came on 23 January 1943, while Roosevelt and Churchill were meeting in Casablanca itself. Many of the extras who sang La Marseillaise in the cafe were real European refugees, and a witness said they were crying.
