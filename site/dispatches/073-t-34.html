Blueprint. Thursday, 19 November 1942 · Stalingrad · Soviet Union

The tank that closed the ring

Its commander had two jobs at once: lead the tank, and aim and fire its gun.

19 November 1942. T-34s led the armored thrusts of Operation Uranus, the offensive that would encircle the German Sixth Army at Stalingrad.

The 1942 model carried a long 76.2 mm gun behind 45 mm of hull armor sloped at 60 degrees, which protected far better than its thickness suggests. A 500 horsepower diesel drove wide tracks that handled dirt roads and open country.

The weak point was the cramped two man turret. It was fixed only in 1944, when the T-34-85's three man turret let the commander simply command.
