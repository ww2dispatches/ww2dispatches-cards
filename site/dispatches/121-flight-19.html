Battle Lines. Wednesday, 5 December 1945 · Fort Lauderdale · United States

Flight 19 never comes home

Lt. Charles Taylor radioed that both his compasses were out. Believing he was over the Florida Keys, he led five bombers further out to sea.

5 December 1945. Flight 19, five TBM Avengers with 14 airmen, had left Fort Lauderdale that afternoon on a training flight over the Bahamas. Night fell and the weather worsened. Taylor's last words were heard at 7:04 p.m.

A PBM Mariner flying boat sent to search, with 13 men aboard, also vanished. No wreckage of any of the six aircraft was ever found.

The Navy blamed Taylor. His mother, Katherine, fought the finding, and in 1947 it was changed to say the flight disappeared "for reasons or causes unknown." The photo shows Navy Avengers in 1942, not the lost flight.

Photo: Five Navy Avengers in formation like Flight 19, near Norfolk, Virginia, September 1942 (not the lost flight). National Archives 80-G-427475, public domain.
