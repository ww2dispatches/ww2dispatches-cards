Home Front. Tuesday, 23 November 1943 · Berlin · Germany

The night Berlin's church burned

Berliners fought to save a ruin. In the 1950s an architect planned to tear down the broken spire of their memorial church, and they would not let him.

22 to 23 November 1943. Overnight, 764 RAF bombers came over Berlin, the largest force yet sent to the city. In dry weather the fires grew into firestorms in the western districts. About 2,000 people were killed and 175,000 lost their homes. The Kaiser Wilhelm Memorial Church burned, leaving the stump of its main spire.

Berliners called the ruin the heart of their city. Egon Eiermann changed his design, and his new church rose beside the old tower. Inside the tower hangs a cross made from nails of Coventry Cathedral, destroyed by German bombers in 1940.
