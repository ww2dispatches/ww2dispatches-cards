Statecraft. Tuesday, 20 November 1945 · Nuremberg · Germany

The Nuremberg trial opens

In the front row of the dock sat Hermann Goering, Rudolf Hess, Joachim von Ribbentrop and Wilhelm Keitel. White helmeted American MPs stood at their backs.

20 November 1945. The International Military Tribunal opened its trial of the major Nazi war criminals in Nuremberg's Palace of Justice, in the old city of the Nazi Party rallies. The United States, Britain, France and the Soviet Union each sat a judge and an alternate, with Lord Justice Geoffrey Lawrence presiding. He told the court the trial was "unique in the history of the jurisprudence of the world."

The whole first day went to reading the indictment aloud, with prosecutors from all four nations taking turns. The next morning the defendants would plead.
