# WW2 Dispatches: Media Guide

## The basics
Every post is a branded card with a real archival photo as the hero. A truly major event can also get a short film clip (about 15 seconds) that keeps the film's own period sound. Only real public domain material is used. Never suggest AI generated images.

## Photo rules
1. Prefer a photo of the event itself, taken that day or during that event.
2. A photo from another time (a portrait, the ship before or after, a museum aircraft) can work, especially for Spec Sheets, but label it "from the day: no."
3. For huge moments, find the iconic shot first, the one people remember. If it is not public domain, say so and give the next best.
4. The photo must be large and clear enough for a phone screen card. Note it if the only copy looks small or blurry.
5. Give the exact page for the item (the record page, not a search results page or a site's home page), plus the record number.
6. No pick should depend on a national insignia as the point of the image.
7. Somber subjects (the Holocaust, civilian deaths) need a respectful, non graphic photo. If none exists, choose another event.

## Good photo sources
* US National Archives (catalog.archives.gov), with the NAID
* Library of Congress (loc.gov), with the call or reproduction number; FSA/OWI photos are ideal for Home Front
* Naval History and Heritage Command (history.navy.mil), with the NH or 80 G number
* US Army Signal Corps photos (SC numbers), US Air Force and USMC official photos
* Imperial War Museum (iwm.org.uk), British official wartime photos only (Crown copyright expired), with the catalogue number like E 18380; skip private and press photos
* Australian War Memorial official photos marked public domain
* Wikimedia Commons, only when the file page shows a clear public domain license and names an original archive source with its number

## Good film sources
* US Army Signal Corps film at NARA (Record Group 111, 111 ADC series), with the NAID
* US Navy film at NARA (428 NPC series)
* Other US government film at NARA
* British official wartime film with expired Crown copyright (IWM)
* Universal Newsreel at NARA (mark "confirm status")

## Avoid
* United News reels (possible commercial copyright)
* British Pathé, Movietone, Getty, AP, Corbis, Alamy
* Colorized, upscaled, or remastered versions
* Any photo with an unclear license or no original source
* YouTube uploads as a source (find the original archive record instead)
