## October 26

### 8 AM Story
Event: Battle of the Santa Cruz Islands. USS Hornet heavily damaged and later abandoned after Japanese air attacks. Enterprise also hit.
Series: Battle Lines
Date confirmed: October 26, 1942 main action. Sources: Naval History and Heritage Command; National Archives Record Group 80.
Photo: USS Enterprise under AA fire, NARA 80-G-20989. Link: https://www.history.navy.mil/content/history/museums/nmusn/explore/photography/wwii/wwii-pacific/wwii-pacific-guadalancanal/naval-battles/santa-cruz-islands.html
Why: High stakes carrier duel, decisive for Guadalcanal air control. Living card candidate: NARA has combat film footage of the action.

### 5 PM Evening card
Format: Spec Sheet
Event: North American P-51 Mustang first flight, October 26, 1940. Prototype NA-73X flown by Vance Breese at Mines Field.
Series: Blueprint
Key numbers (two sources): Completed in 102 days from order; max speed about 390 mph with Allison engine (Britannica; historyofwar.org timeline). Range extended later with drop tanks.
Photo: Early Mustang formation or prototype images in public domain via NARA/USAF collections.
Why: Clever engineering response to British request, shaped later air war.

### Backup
Event: Hornet abandoned on this date after damage. Same battle, usable abandon ship photo 80-G-34110 from NHHC.
