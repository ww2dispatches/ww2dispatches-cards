On 26 November 1941, six Japanese aircraft carriers sailed into the empty North Pacific and went silent. Eleven days later their planes were over Oahu.

Swipe through the four days that set it in motion. The fleet slips out of the Kurils. Washington sends a war warning that names the Philippines, Thailand and Borneo, but not Hawaii. One American carrier sails for Wake already at war. And in Tokyo, eight former prime ministers get one last chance to speak.
