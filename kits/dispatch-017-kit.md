## YouTube Shorts description
On 25 October 1944, off the island of Samar, six small escort carriers and their seven little escorts, called Taffy 3, found Admiral Kurita's Center Force bearing down on them: four battleships, including the giant Yamato, plus cruisers and destroyers. The destroyers and destroyer escorts charged, the carrier planes attacked again and again, and at 9:20 Kurita broke off the battle.

This is real U.S. Navy film from that morning: a plane launching from an escort carrier, then Japanese shell splashes rising among the ships of Taffy 3. The original film is silent.

Film: U.S. Navy, "Battle of Philippines: USS Kalinin Bay burning", 428-NPC-6071, U.S. National Archives (catalog.archives.gov/id/77765), public domain.
Sources: Battle off Samar, Wikipedia; USS Johnston (DD-557), Naval History and Heritage Command.
