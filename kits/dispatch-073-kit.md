## YouTube Shorts description
T-34s like these went first when the Red Army opened Operation Uranus on 19 November 1942, and their drive left the German Sixth Army cut off at Stalingrad. Nothing the Romanian troops in their path carried was heavier than a 47 mm antitank gun.

By 1942 the T-34 carried the long 76.2 mm F-34 gun behind hull armor 45 mm thick, sloped at 60 degrees, which protected it far better than the thickness alone suggests. A 500 horsepower V-2 diesel drove it on wide tracks that coped with dirt roads and open country. Its weak point was the cramped two man turret, where the commander doubled as the gunner. That was fixed only in 1944.

This is Soviet film of T-34s carrying infantry into the Uranus offensive, November 1942, from the Soviet documentary Stalingrad (1943). The original film carries narration and music, so this clip runs silent.
