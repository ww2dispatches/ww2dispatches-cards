## YouTube Shorts description
On 20 November 1945 the International Military Tribunal opened its trial of the major Nazi war criminals in the Palace of Justice at Nuremberg, the old city of the Nazi Party rallies. Judges from the United States, Britain, France, and the Soviet Union sat on the bench, with Lord Justice Geoffrey Lawrence presiding. The whole first day went to reading the indictment aloud, prosecutors from all four nations taking turns. Every word was interpreted at once into English, Russian, French, and German by 36 interpreters working in three teams of 12, and listeners picked a language by switching channels on IBM headsets. The next morning came the pleas and the American opening; the verdicts came on 1 October 1946.

This is U.S. Army film of the Nuremberg courtroom from the trial's opening days, November 1945: the bench, an interpreter, the dock under guard, and defendants on headphones. It is not film of 20 November itself. The clip runs silent.

Film: Nuremberg (1948, U.S. Army), 111-M-7596 reel 1, U.S. National Archives (catalog.archives.gov/id/35957), public domain.
