## YouTube Shorts description
Just after sunrise on 1 November 1943, the 3rd Marine Division began landing at Cape Torokina on Empress Augusta Bay, Bougainville, in Operation Cherryblossom. Almost 7,500 Marines made the run to 12 beaches through heavy surf, and a single Japanese 75mm gun raked the boats. The goal was ground for airfields: Rabaul, Japan's main base in the region, lay less than 250 miles away. By the end of the day about 14,000 men were ashore.

This is real U.S. Navy film from Cape Torokina, dated 1 November 1943: Marines with packs and rifles moving along the beach. The original film is silent.

Film: U.S. Navy, 428-NPC-1350, U.S. National Archives (catalog.archives.gov/id/75959), public domain.
