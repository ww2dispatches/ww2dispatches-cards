## YouTube Shorts description
On 17 November 1944 the 442nd Regimental Combat Team, the Army's Japanese American regiment, pulled back to Docelles and was released from the 36th Infantry Division after more than a month of fighting in the Vosges Mountains of eastern France. In October it had liberated Bruyeres, taken Biffontaine, and from 26 to 30 October fought through the forest to rescue the 211 surviving men of the 141st Infantry's Lost Battalion. The regiment's journal counts 140 killed and 1,800 other casualties in the Vosges. By 8 November Company K had 17 riflemen, led by a sergeant. At Docelles the men finally got hot showers, and 382 new replacements joined. On 19 November the combat team left the Vosges by truck for the French and Italian border in the Maritime Alps.

This is real U.S. Army Signal Corps film shot on 17 November 1944 in the St Die sector: an 81 mm mortar crew of the 442nd Combat Team's 2nd Battalion at the front line. The original film is silent.

Film: U.S. Army Signal Corps, 111-ADC-2719, U.S. National Archives (catalog.archives.gov/id/16523), public domain.
