## YouTube Shorts description
On 7 November 1944, President Franklin D. Roosevelt won a fourth term, defeating Thomas E. Dewey with 432 electoral votes to 99. It was the closest of his four presidential elections, held in the middle of the war, with millions of service members voting by absentee ballot. His new running mate was Senator Harry S. Truman. Roosevelt is the only president ever elected four times; less than three months after his inauguration he died at Warm Springs, Georgia.

This is real U.S. Army Signal Corps film shot the next day, 8 November 1944, in Moyeuvre, France: soldiers of the 95th Signal Company parade behind a jeep carrying a sign that reads "Roosevelt Elected." The Army's film notes call the soldiers' show of joy "very stagey." The original film is silent.

Film: U.S. Army Signal Corps, 111-ADC-2651, U.S. National Archives (catalog.archives.gov/id/16455), public domain.
Sources: Election of 1944, The American Presidency Project; The Soldier Voting Act and Absentee Ballots in World War II, The National WWII Museum; Amendment XXII, National Constitution Center.
