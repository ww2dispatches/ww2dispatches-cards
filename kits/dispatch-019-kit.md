## YouTube Shorts description
On 26 October 1942, near the Santa Cruz Islands, American and Japanese carriers traded air strikes in the long fight for Guadalcanal. USS Hornet, the carrier that had carried the Doolittle Raiders six months earlier, was hit by bombs, torpedoes, and a crashing dive bomber. She would not sink under American torpedoes and gunfire, and Japanese destroyers finished her that night.

This is real U.S. Navy film of the battle: a bomb bursting beside a carrier deck crowded with planes, then a burning carrier dead in the water and taken under tow. The original film is silent.

Film: U.S. Navy, "Battle of Santa Cruz Islands", 428-NPC-13399, U.S. National Archives (catalog.archives.gov/id/79581), public domain.
Sources: The United States loses the Hornet, HISTORY; Battle of the Santa Cruz Islands, History of War.
