## YouTube Shorts description
At 07:20 Moscow time on 19 November 1942, Soviet gunners on the Don northwest of Stalingrad received the code word "Siren". Ten minutes later about 3,500 guns and mortars opened fire through fog and snow. At 08:50 the 5th Tank and 21st Armies attacked the Romanian Third Army, whose antitank guns were no heavier than 47 mm; its line broke by noon. That night Army Group B stopped all German attacks inside Stalingrad. On 20 November a second blow came from south of the city, and on 23 November the two pincers met near Kalach on the Don, trapping the German Sixth Army and part of the Fourth Panzer Army. Hitler ordered them to hold and be supplied by air. The Sixth Army surrendered at the start of February 1943.

This is Soviet newsreel film of the Stalingrad counteroffensive, November 1942, as used in the U.S. War Department film The Battle of Russia (1943). The shots are not dated to 19 November. The U.S. film carries narration and music, so this clip runs silent.

Film: The Battle of Russia (U.S. War Department, 1943), 111-OF-5 reel 10, U.S. National Archives (catalog.archives.gov/id/36071), public domain.
