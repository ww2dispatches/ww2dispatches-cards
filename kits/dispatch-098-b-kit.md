## YouTube Shorts description
A Soviet guard and band march past the embassy where Roosevelt was a house guest. Marshall and Arnold missed the first session: they were out touring Tehran.

The Tehran Conference, code named Eureka, ran from 28 November to 1 December 1943. It was the first time Franklin D. Roosevelt, Winston Churchill and Joseph Stalin met together. At 3:00 p.m. Roosevelt, Admiral William Leahy, Harry Hopkins and Major John Boettiger drove to the Soviet Embassy to live there as guests of the Soviet government. Stalin lived in a smaller house in the same compound, and the British Legation was a block away. Generals George Marshall and Henry Arnold missed that first meeting. It had been called at very short notice, and both were out on an auto tour of Tehran. The payoff: before the conference ended, the British and Americans committed to Operation Overlord, the invasion of northern France, by May 1944, and the Soviets agreed to a major offensive in the east to hold German troops away from it.

This is film from the Soviet Embassy grounds during the Tehran Conference, November 1943: a Soviet guard and band marching past, conferees gathering at the steps, and officers on the portico. The original film is silent.

Film: Cairo and Teheran Conferences, November to December 1943, FDR Library Motion Pictures 71-8:62, U.S. National Archives (catalog.archives.gov/id/124450702).
