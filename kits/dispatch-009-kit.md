## YouTube Shorts description
On 21 October 1944, Aachen became the first German city to fall to the Allies, after weeks of fighting through its ruins house by house.

This is real film shot in Aachen on 15 October 1944 by U.S. Army Signal Corps cameramen, six days before the surrender: an M-10 tank destroyer firing point blank into a building, infantry climbing over walls between houses, and a crew pushing a 57mm antitank gun through the streets. The original film is silent.

Film: U.S. Army Signal Corps, 111-ADC-2654, U.S. National Archives (catalog.archives.gov/id/16458), public domain.
Sources: Battle of Aachen, Wikipedia; Signal Corps shot card for 111-ADC-2654.
