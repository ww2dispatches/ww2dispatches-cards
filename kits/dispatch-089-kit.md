## YouTube Shorts title
The Moment a Kamikaze Hit USS Essex, 25 November 1944 #shorts

## YouTube Shorts description
Watch the burning plane come down onto USS Essex. It hit among aircraft fueled for takeoff, and 15 men were killed.

Around noon on 25 November 1944, Task Force 38's carriers were launching strikes on Luzon from about 160 miles east of Manila when about a dozen Japanese planes slipped past the fighter patrol, coming in low and fast. At 12:50 a Jill torpedo plane crashed onto USS Intrepid's flight deck. At 12:58 a second plane crashed into her flight deck amidships. The two hits killed 69 of Intrepid's men and set serious fires that trapped sailors in smoke filled compartments. Her fire marshal, Lt. Donald DiMarzo, organized and led the fire parties on the hangar deck through the flames and explosions. He was killed fighting the fires and was awarded the Navy Cross after his death; his citation says he helped save his ship. Intrepid stayed on station, and her fires were out within two hours. USS Essex was hit on the port edge of her flight deck by a plane flown by Lt. Yoshinori Yamaguchi. It landed among aircraft fueled for takeoff, killing 15 and wounding 44. At about 13:00 a burning kamikaze hit the light carrier USS Cabot's flight deck and another crashed close aboard. 62 of her men were killed or wounded, but she kept her place in formation.

This is U.S. Navy film of the kamikaze hit on USS Essex, 25 November 1944, shot by the combat photo unit of USS Ticonderoga. The original film is silent.
