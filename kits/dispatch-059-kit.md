## YouTube Shorts description
Operation Typhoon, the German drive on Moscow, had stalled in the autumn mud. When frost hardened the ground in mid November 1941, Army Group Centre attacked again: on the morning of 15 November the German Ninth Army struck south of Kalinin, and the next day two panzer groups joined in north of the city. Moscow had been under a state of siege since 20 October, and hundreds of thousands of Muscovites, most of them women, dug antitank ditches around it. General Georgy Zhukov's Western Front received about 100,000 men, 300 tanks and 2,000 guns in the first half of November. On 19 November Stalin asked Zhukov whether Moscow could be held; Zhukov said it would be, but that he needed reserves. German tanks came within 20 miles of the Kremlin before the attack ran out of strength in deep cold, and in early December the Red Army struck back.

This is Soviet film of the defense of Moscow in the autumn and winter of 1941, from the documentary Defeat of the German Armies near Moscow (1942). The shots are not dated to 15 November. The film carries narration and music, so this clip runs silent.
