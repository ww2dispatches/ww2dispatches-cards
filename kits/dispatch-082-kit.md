## YouTube Shorts description
Roosevelt promised Chiang Kai-shek a big landing across the Bay of Bengal. On 5 December he agreed to cancel it.

Franklin D. Roosevelt's plane landed at Cairo West airport at 9.35 a.m. on 22 November 1943, after flying in over the Sphinx and the Pyramids. That afternoon Winston Churchill, Generalissimo Chiang Kai-shek and Madame Chiang called on Roosevelt. It was the first day of talks between the three leaders. Madame Chiang, Soong Mei-ling, interpreted in Roosevelt's talks with her husband. Roosevelt promised Chiang a large amphibious attack across the Bay of Bengal, Operation Buccaneer, a landing on the Andaman Islands, to support the fight in Burma. At noon on 25 November the leaders met in the garden of Roosevelt's villa and posed for military photographers, still and movie cameras together. That is the famous seated group photo. After Tehran, Churchill pressed to drop Buccaneer. Roosevelt held out, then agreed on 5 December to cancel it, taking back the promise he had made to Chiang.

This is U.S. War Department film of the Cairo Conference garden session at Roosevelt's villa on 25 November 1943, three days after the talks opened: Madame Chiang with Mountbatten, Chiang and Roosevelt seated side by side, and the full group with Churchill. The source film carries narration and music, so this clip runs silent.

Film: The Battle of China (U.S. War Department, 1944), 111-OF-6 reel 7, U.S. National Archives (catalog.archives.gov/id/36072), public domain.
