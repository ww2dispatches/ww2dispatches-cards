Under the Radar: Japan Hits the B-29 Base on Saipan #shorts

## YouTube Shorts description
Zeros skimmed the waves to stay under the radar and caught B-29s on the ground at Isley Field. This is what the raids left burning.

Three days after B-29s from the Marianas bombed Tokyo for the first time, on 24 November 1944, the Imperial Japanese Navy struck back at the bombers' home, Isley Field on Saipan. Early on the morning of 27 November, two twin engine Betty bombers from Iwo Jima came in low. They caught Superfortresses being loaded with bombs for the second Tokyo mission, destroyed one B-29 and damaged eleven. At noon came a second strike: Zero fighters of the 252nd Air Group, each carrying a bomb, guided toward Saipan by two Myrt reconnaissance planes. Historic Wings describes it as a one way mission. They flew low over the sea to stay under the American radar. One Zero clipped a wave with its propeller, dropped its bomb and was shot down by Thunderbolts near Pagan. The rest slipped through and swept over Isley and Kobler fields with complete surprise. They destroyed three B-29s and badly damaged two more. The defenders shot the raiders down one by one: AAF fighters got four and antiaircraft gunners six. In the confusion the gunners also shot down an American P-47, a loss the official report called "inexcusable". The last Japanese pilot, out of ammunition, landed his Zero on Isley Field and opened fire on the Americans with his pistol until infantry rifle fire killed him. Brigadier General Haywood Hansell, commander of XXI Bomber Command, saw it happen. The raids came from Iwo Jima. After 27 November, Admiral Nimitz ordered a heavy strike on the island, and the bombing of Iwo Jima went on until the Marines landed there on 19 February 1945.

This is U.S. Army Signal Corps film of the Japanese raids on Isley Field, Saipan, in November 1944: fires on the airfield at night, a bulldozer at the wreckage, and a burning B-29. The bulletin does not date the shots on screen. The original film carries narration, so this clip runs silent.
