## YouTube Shorts title
"I Have Returned": MacArthur Lands on Leyte, 1944

## YouTube Shorts description
On 20 October 1944, General Douglas MacArthur came ashore at Red Beach on Leyte, keeping the promise he made when he was ordered out of the Philippines in 1942. On the beach he spoke into a radio microphone: "People of the Philippines, I have returned!"

This U.S. Army Signal Corps film shows MacArthur in a landing craft with President Sergio Osmeña and his staff, the boats at the beach, and the party moving inland.

Film: U.S. Army Signal Corps reel 111-ADC-3205, National Archives, https://catalog.archives.gov/id/17008 (public domain).
Sources: National Archives DocsTeach; Battle of Leyte, Wikipedia.
