## YouTube Shorts title
Carrier Planes Strike Manila Bay: Real Film, 5 November 1944 #shorts

## YouTube Shorts description
On 5 November 1944, two weeks after the Battle of Leyte Gulf, U.S. Navy carrier planes struck Japanese airfields on Luzon and shipping in Manila Bay. The new carrier USS Ticonderoga launched her first strike that morning. The heavy cruiser Nachi, Vice Adm. Kiyohide Shima's flagship, tried to flee the bay at high speed; planes from Essex and Lexington sank her near Corregidor, and about 807 of her crew were killed. Shima watched from shore. That afternoon a burning Japanese plane crashed into Lexington's island, and about 50 of her men were killed.

This is real U.S. Navy film shot aboard Ticonderoga that day; the cameraman's slate reads "Combat Photo Unit No. 12, D. R. Brown, CV-14 / Manila, 11/5/44." The original film is silent. Music: "War Epic" by Paul Yudin (Pixabay).

Film: U.S. Navy, 428-NPC-6204, U.S. National Archives (catalog.archives.gov/id/77794), public domain.
Sources: IJN Nachi Tabular Record of Movement, Combinedfleet.com; Lexington V (CV-16), Dictionary of American Naval Fighting Ships, Naval History and Heritage Command; USS Ticonderoga (CV-14), Wikipedia.
