## YouTube Shorts description
On 18 November 1944 troops of the US Third Army closed on Metz, an old fortress city in Lorraine ringed by forts. The 5th Infantry Division pushed in from the south while the 95th came from the north and west, and men of the 10th and 11th Infantry crossed the city limits that day. Early that morning the Germans blew the Moselle bridges west of the city; one went up under a patrol of the 378th Infantry, killing eight men. On 19 November the two divisions linked up inside the ring. There was no real house to house battle: most Germans outside the forts fired a few shots and gave up. Fighting in the city formally ended on 22 November, but the outer forts were left to starve, and the last, Fort Jeanne d'Arc, held out until 13 December.

This is real U.S. Army Signal Corps film shot on 18 November 1944, the 'Capture of Metz' story: P-47s dive bombing near the city, infantry in a village street, and a soldier at a gateway. The original film is silent.
