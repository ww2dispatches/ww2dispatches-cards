## Summary
The week split in two. Through Thursday 5 Nov the account had its best days yet: Rommel's retreat from Alamein became the best card ever (Threads 3,210 views, Reach 8.36), Manila Bay (3.47), the Fu-Go balloon Spec Sheet (2.91), and the Spruce Goose Spec Sheet (2.95, 12 Threads comments) all ran far above typical, and Threads drew 3,566 views on 4 Nov. Then, from the 5 p.m. post on 5 Nov, Threads views fell by about 80 percent across the board: about 300 a day from 6 to 9 Nov against 1,400 to 3,500 before, with five of the last eight scored cards under 60 Threads views. It hit every series, format, and slot alike, so it looks like Threads cutting the account's distribution, not the subjects failing; 10 Nov shows a partial recovery (Ike in the mud, 569 views in a day). Because Threads is the only card platform that counts, this week's late cards score low for reasons outside the card. Videos held steady on TikTok and Instagram, YouTube had its best week (9,241 views in the week from 1 Nov), but Facebook Reels went quiet again (four in a row near zero) and the Torch video drew only 17 YouTube views. One finding reached the pattern rung: Battle Lines cards out reach Blueprint and Home Front cards, for the third review running.
