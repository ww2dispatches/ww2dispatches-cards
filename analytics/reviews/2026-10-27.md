# Weekly review, Tue 27 Oct 2026

The first real review. Snapshot `analytics/snapshots/2026-10-27.json` (158 Buffer posts sent 16 to 27 Oct). 33 posts matched, 22 scored (6 final, 16 provisional), 8 too early (sent Sunday to Tuesday). Everything below is a hunch: one review, and most groups are under 5 posts.

## Summary
Threads is still where the cards live: a typical card there now draws 456 views, and the best evening posts drew three to four times that. The strongest cards were a famous face or a famous voice (Tojo's portrait, Churchill's broadcast to France) and big turning point mornings (Aachen, Mark Clark's secret landing, MacArthur at Leyte). Videos now reach more people than cards on every platform except Threads: YouTube Shorts gave the Living Cards 800 to 1,600 views each and TikTok 300 to 700, while the two narrated Story Cards drew almost nothing on YouTube (36 and 18 views). Quiet procedural subjects (the tin can drive, the Nuremberg indictment, the UN Charter) and Spec Sheets drew least on Threads. Instagram, X, and Facebook cards still sit below 20 views each and cannot be judged. Two odd results need a look: the Alamein morning card and the Musashi Name This drew only 11 and 6 views on Threads, and Facebook Reels fell from about 300 views each (22 to 24 Oct) to almost none on 25 and 26 Oct.

* **No. 010, Churchill speaks to France** (Quote of the Day): Threads 1,614 views, 90 reactions, 6 reposts, Reach 3.5, Response 3.3. The best all round card so far. A famous voice, a hopeful line people want to share, on the anniversary of the broadcast.
* **No. 006, the WAVES open to Black women** (Photo of the Day): Response 5.5, by far the highest. Still the clearest sign that barrier breaking stories about people often left out draw real feeling. One post only.
* **No. 009, Aachen surrenders**, card and Living Card: card Reach 2.5 on Threads; the video drew 1,608 on YouTube, 1,472 on Threads, 727 on TikTok (33 percent hold). A first (first German city to fall) with clear film.
* **No. 013, El Alamein Living Card**: best video hold (45 percent on TikTok, 38 on Instagram) and 1,360 YouTube views, even though it opens nearly black on night footage. That cuts against last week's worry about the dark opening.
* **Name This, Tirpitz** (18 Oct): 2,364 Threads views and 5 comments, the most viewed post of the fortnight.
