## Playbook items applied
* Battle Lines at 8 a.m. when the date allows (pattern): five of seven 8 a.m. slots are Battle Lines; the two Statecraft weekend slots exist because Jackson's opening has his own voice on film.
* Combat and turning points, and following a campaign to its climax: Operation Queen across Monday and Tuesday, Tarawa across Friday and Saturday, Moscow and Stalingrad on the Eastern Front.
* For videos, the film decides: every 8 a.m. pick has usable film; fourteen Living Cards, no Story Cards.
* Groups often left out: the 442nd's last days in the Vosges (Tue 8 a.m.), a follow up to the Lost Battalion.
* A human story inside a machine: Udet (the failure behind the Luftwaffe's aircraft), the mice in the 22nd Panzer's tanks, Washington's radar gunnery.
* A famous leader at a dramatic moment: Jackson's opening at Nuremberg, a line people already half know.
* Faces for Photo of the Day where a public domain face existed (064 has none; 067 and 070 show soldiers and tank crews).
