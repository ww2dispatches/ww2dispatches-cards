# Slates

One file per week, named for the Sunday the week starts (YYYY-MM-DD.md), written by the Wednesday research run. Each lists the 14 locked slots with their numbers, events, planned video kind, film and photo status, and one or two alternates under each 8 a.m. slot. The dispatch JSON is the record; the slate is the picture of the week for Brian. Rules: docs/WW2 Dispatches Research.md.
