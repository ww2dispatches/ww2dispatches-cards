# Slate: Sunday 22 to Saturday 28 November 2026

Written by the Wednesday research run on 18 Nov 2026 (Mountain). Numbers 080 to 100 are locked. The dispatch JSON in `dispatches/` is the record; this is the picture of the week. Swap any day, or the newsletter story, by telling Claude in the Project before Thursday 19 Nov, 7 p.m. Mountain; the number stays with the slot.

The Pearl Harbor Campaign Series posts at 6:30 a.m. from Thu 26 Nov (Hitokappu Bay and the Hull note, the war warning, Enterprise sailing), so none of those are card subjects this week. Thanksgiving is Thu 26 Nov. All 14 videos are Living Cards on film secured on R2; no Story Cards and nothing needs Brian's computer.

Theaters this week: Eastern Front (Ladoga, Paulus), Western Europe (Strasbourg, Mosquito), Mediterranean and North Africa (Rommel, Barham, Rohna, Toulon), Atlantic (Rawalpindi), Pacific (B-29s over Tokyo, the kamikazes, O'Hare, the Saipan raid, Shinano), worldwide (Cairo, Tehran), home front (Berlin, Casablanca, Fauld, Cocoanut Grove).
