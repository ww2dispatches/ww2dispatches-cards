## Sun 29 Nov

**No. 101 · 8:00 a.m. · Battle Lines** · Grosshau falls by moonlight (1944) · `1944-11-29-grosshau`
* Story test: Col. Buck Lanham's careful flanking plan is overruled at noon ("take it today"); his worn out battalion stalls, the flankers reach the ridge at dusk, and more than 100 Germans surrender by moonlight. Hemingway was with the regiment.
* Noon video: **Living Card**. Film: Signal Corps 111-ADC-2798 (NARA 16601), 4.5 inch rocket batteries firing in the Hürtgen Forest, slated 29 Nov 1944 on screen (06:39 to 08:24), silent, `footage/1944-11-29-hurtgen-adc2798.mp4`.
* Lead photo: downloaded. Weary 4th Division infantrymen in the Hürtgen, SC 196619, 18 Nov 1944, public domain (8th Infantry, not the 22nd; no photo of Grosshau itself found).
* Somber: no. Theater: Western Europe. Facts: 11, sources: 5.
* Alternates:
  * Merode, 29 Nov to 1 Dec 1944: two companies of the 26th Infantry cut off and lost. Same Signal Corps reel family; somber.
  * Coffee rationing begins in the U.S., 29 Nov 1942. Home Front; no film found, a Story Card.

**No. 102 · 2:30 p.m. · Statecraft** · Todt tells Hitler: no victory (1941) · `1941-11-29-todt`
* Axis card, story card. Lens: axis_germany. Nation: Germany.
* Story test: armaments minister Fritz Todt and industrialist Walter Rohland tell Hitler the war can no longer be won by arms; twelve days later Hitler declares war on the United States, and Todt dies in a crash at Rastenburg in Feb 1942.
* Lead photo: downloaded. Todt, March 1940, Bundesarchiv Bild 146-1969-146-01, CC BY-SA 3.0 (soft, 600 px original; crop to the face, party uniform).
* Somber: no. Theater: home front. Facts: 12, sources: 4.
* Open: the quoted words come from Rohland's memoir only, and the card says so. (A first draft on Rostov was dropped: it repeated No. 069.)

**No. 103 · 5:00 p.m. · Blueprint · Photo of the Day** · The guns that guarded Antwerp (1944) · `1944-11-29-antwerp-guns`
* Story test: Montgomery asked Brig. Gen. Clare Armstrong to stop half the V-1s; Armstrong was not satisfied with 50 percent, and by March his guns downed 89 of 91 in six days.
* 7 p.m. video: **Living Card** on this card. Film: the same reel 111-ADC-2798, "AA battery knocks out buzz bombs, 29 Nov 1944" (01:55 to 04:12): a 90mm gun and M9 director firing, quad .50s, a director painted with kill marks, the radar. No shot shared with noon.
* Lead photo: downloaded. A "buzz bomb buster" gun in the snow in Belgium, NARA 342-FH-3A05160, public domain (winter 1944 to 1945).
* Somber: no. Theater: Western Europe. Facts: 11, sources: 7.
