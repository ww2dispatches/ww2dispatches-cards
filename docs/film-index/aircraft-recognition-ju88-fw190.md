# Aircraft Recognition films: the Ju 88 and the Fw 190 (1943)

British training films made by the Army Kinematograph Service with the Admiralty and Air Ministry, issued to U.S. forces as Army training films (record group 111-TF). Crown copyright, expired. British narration; run silent.

## Recognizing the Ju 88 (111-TF-1-3684, naId 6044531, 7 min 18 s)
* **00:54 to 01:30: German film.** Ju 88s on a German airfield with crews at work, a takeoff, a formation seen from another bomber, bombs falling. Caption: "German film, from the British training film Recognizing the Ju 88 (1943)".
* 02:06 to 03:06: clean air to air passes of a Ju 88 (likely a captured aircraft flown for the camera). White graphic outlines at 03:12 to 03:48; avoid.
* Use: Blueprint (the Ju 88), Blitz and Battle of Britain dates as undated illustration.

## Recognizing the Focke-Wulf 190 (111-TF-3681, naId 6044534, 6 min 56 s)
* The Fw 190 the RAF captured intact on 23 Jun 1942 (landed in Wales by mistake; verify), flown for the camera. Allied film of a German aircraft. Caption: "A captured Fw 190 filmed by the British, 1943".
* Clean: 01:00 to 01:36, 01:42 to 02:06, 02:36 to 03:00, 03:12 to 03:30. Graphics at 02:12 to 02:30, 03:06, 03:36; avoid.
* Use: Blueprint (the Fw 190, its BMW 801 radial), 23 Jun 1942.
