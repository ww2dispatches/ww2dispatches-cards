# Captured German reels at NARA, batch 2 (19 Nov 2026, evening)

Under Brian's 19 Nov 2026 rule: NARA's own copy only, captioned "German film, captured, U.S. National Archives", picture only (never the German narration, music or speech), never presented as fact, NARA identifier in `film.record`, `film.license` "captured German film, NARA". Times are file times in NARA's MP4. **Dates below come from what is on screen and the general record; verify each before captioning a day.** Contact sheets were checked every 6 seconds; check every clip at full size before cutting, and skip any frame with bodies.

34 files sheeted: every digitized NARA reel titled as German film, German newsreel or captured German film in 428-NPC, 111-ADC, 342-USAF and record group 242. Batch 1 (GN 88, 242-MID-6310, Volk im Krieg) is in `captured-german-reels-2026-11-19.md`.

## Checked and left out
* **111-ADC-9886, "Germaansche Vrijwilligers in de Waffen-SS":** a Dutch language SS recruiting film. Out under the Axis lens guardrails (never the SS told from its own side).
* **FC-FC-4219:** U-35 in the First World War (1917). Out of scope.
* **428-NPC-4912, 4913 (OSS-263), 428-NPC-5583, 428-NPC-16781:** dark or blown out VHS copies with burned in timecode. Not postable.
* **111-ADC-2501:** a duplicate of 428-NPC-16764 with a 1944 U.S. ceremony added.
* **428-NPC-22716** (naId 81775): a German naval film of torpedo boats ("Torpedoboot Greif") at sea, with torpedo runs and gun drill. It looks like a staged prewar or training film; usable only as Blueprint pictures of a torpedo boat, captioned as a German naval film, never as combat.
