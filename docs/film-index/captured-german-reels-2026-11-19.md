# Captured German reels at NARA, first batch (19 Nov 2026)

Indexed under Brian's 19 Nov 2026 rule: NARA's own captured German film may be used, from NARA's copy only, captioned "German film, captured, U.S. National Archives", picture only, never presented as fact. Record the NARA identifier in `film.record` and set `film.license` to "captured German film, NARA". Run silent; the German narration and music are never kept.

A sweep of the NARA catalog for record group 242 moving images found 385 records, but **only 7 are digitized**. Three are German and were sheeted here. The other four are Italian (Giornale di Guerra, out), Soviet (Russian Air Show, a 1949 Belarus musical) and a Spanish copy of a Japanese film (Nanking 1937, with dead in the streets; out). The rest of record group 242 film would need a NARA reproduction order. The captured German film in 428-NPC, 111-ADC and 342-USAF (listed in `german-footage-sources-2026-11-13.md`, Section 4) is the next place to look; much of it is digitized.

## 1. German News No. 88 (242-GN-88, naId 43608, 10 min 0 s) — the best of the batch
https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/mopix/242/GN/242-gn-88.mp4
German overseas newsreel, September 1943 (Italy's armistice and the German takeover). Sharp copy.
| Time | Shows | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 00:06 to 01:30 | German paratroopers fighting on the road into a town and along a coast, smoke and burning vehicles, men with MP 40s | 9 to 10 Sep 1943, the German seizure of Rome (verify place; NARA's note says northern Italy and Rome) |
| 01:36 to 02:06 | German troops in Rome, an eagle crest on a gate, Roman women at a window, a truck passing the Colosseum (02:06) | 10 to 11 Sep 1943, Germans occupy Rome |
| 02:12 to 02:48 | A German bomber over hills, aircrew, an aerial view of ships under attack with letter marks (02:42 to 02:48) | Possibly the attack on the Italian fleet, 9 Sep 1943 (Roma sunk); verify before captioning it as that |
| 02:54 to 04:12 | Coastal fighting: an anti tank gun crew, a StuG assault gun in smoke (03:24 to 03:30), infantry rushing a wall | Salerno, 9 to 16 Sep 1943 (verify) |
| 04:18 to 05:36 | The Gran Sasso: trucks on a mountain road, paratroopers, the Campo Imperatore hotel (05:12 to 05:18), German troops in camouflage smocks | **12 Sep 1943, the raid that freed Mussolini** |
| 05:36 to 07:00 | Mussolini in black coat and hat among the raiders, the Storch on the slope (06:42), Mussolini boards it (06:48 to 06:54) | 12 Sep 1943 |
| 07:06 to 07:30 | A He 111 and a Ju 52 on an airfield, a plane overhead | 12 Sep 1943, the flight to Vienna and on |
| 07:36 to 09:30 | **Hitler meets Mussolini** at the plane and at his headquarters, handshakes, cars | **14 Sep 1943, Rastenburg** |
* Staging caution: the Gran Sasso film was shot by a German propaganda cameraman on the raid; it is real but built to glorify it. Caption the facts plainly (no "daring" or "brilliant").
* No bodies seen on the sheets; check 05:06 (a man lying on a slope) at full size.

## 3. Volk im Krieg (242-MID-100, naId 43462, 22 min 36 s)
https://catalog.archives.gov/medialz/mopix/242/MID/242-100.mp4
A 1940 German film on the nation at war (with a "Die Waffenkammer Deutschlands" section at 17:48). Strong factory film: steel pouring, gun barrels machined, shells by the thousand (11:12 to 11:48), rifles, Bf 109 assembly, aircraft in formation (10:18 to 10:24), Panzer IIs in a forest (10:30 to 10:42), a battleship at sea. Also harvests, sport and Berlin entertainment (16:00 to 17:24).
* Pure propaganda about plenty and strength; use only for what the picture shows (how a gun barrel was made, what a shell line looked like), never for the message.
* Serves Blueprint and Home Front (German side).
