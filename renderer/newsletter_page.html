Paste and schedule (about 10 minutes)

  1. Start a new post in Substack

    Best on the desktop PC. Sign in to Substack, then:

    New post in Substack

    If that link opens the dashboard instead, tap Create new, then Text post.

  2. Title

    Paste this into the Title box. It becomes the email subject.

  3. Subtitle

    Paste this into the Subtitle box. It's the preview line people see in their inbox.

  4. Body

    Click into the body and paste. The headings, photos, captions, and links come with it.

    If the photos don't show up after pasting, delete the empty spots and add each photo with Substack's image button. Use Copy image or Open full image under each photo in Review. Each caption should land in the photo's own caption line (small text under the picture). If one pastes as a separate paragraph instead, open the photo's ··· menu, choose Edit caption, paste it there, and delete the paragraph, so the drop cap stays on the story's first paragraph.

  5. Check it

    Scroll through once: every photo shows with its caption, and the links open. Optional: use Preview, then Send test email, to see it in your inbox.

  6. Tags and subscribe buttons

    Tap Continue. Under Add tags, pick these two (create them the first time):

    When Substack offers to add subscribe buttons, tap Add subscribe buttons. If one lands inside the story, drag it down to just before the links list at the end. Keep the title exactly as above, since the Sunday share post links to it.

  7. Schedule it

    Audience Everyone, send by email on. Choose Schedule and set , 7:00 a.m. Check the time zone shows Mountain, then confirm.

  8. Mark it scheduled

    Tap below, then mark the "Paste and schedule the weekly newsletter" task done on the Schedule Board.
