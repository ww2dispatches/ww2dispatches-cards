Paste and schedule (about 10 minutes)

  1. Start a new post in Substack

    Best on the desktop PC. Sign in to Substack, then:

    New post in Substack

    If that link opens the dashboard instead, tap Create new, then Text post.

  2. Title

    Paste this into the Title box. It becomes the email subject.

  3. Subtitle

    Paste this into the Subtitle box. It's the preview line people see in their inbox.

  4. Body

    Click into the body and paste. The headings, links, and card images come with it.

    If the cards don't show up after pasting, delete the empty spots and add each card with Substack's image button. Use Copy card or Open full card under each card in Review.

  5. Check it

    Scroll through once: every dispatch has its card, and the X links open. Optional: use Preview, then Send test email, to see it in your inbox.

  6. Schedule it

    Tap Continue. Audience Everyone, send by email on. Choose Schedule and set , 8:00 a.m. Check the time zone shows Mountain, then confirm.

  7. Mark it scheduled

    Tap below, then mark the "Paste and schedule the weekly newsletter" task done on the Schedule Board.
